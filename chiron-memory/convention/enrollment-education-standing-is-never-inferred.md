Enrollment/education standing is never inferred from résumé parsing

## Why
a résumé states past dates, not whether the candidate is enrolled today; inferring "still studying" from an open-ended entry would be exactly the guess the eligibility filter is meant to eliminate

## Where
résumé extraction pipeline vs. profile's education standing fields
