Epic-level acceptance verification is written up as a dedicated report doc under `docs/` (pattern: `docs/epic-NN-acceptance-check.md`), stating scope, gaps found, and confirmed non-issues; this task followed the same pattern with `docs/sensitive-field-enforcement-check.md`.

## Why
keeps a durable, human-readable record of what was checked and what was explicitly out of scope, independent of the test suite itself.

## Where
docs/epic-05-acceptance-check.md, docs/sensitive-field-enforcement-check.md.
