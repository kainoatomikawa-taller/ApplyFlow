Architectural decisions are recorded as numbered ADRs under `docs/decisions/` (e.g. `0001-application-shell-web-vs-cli.md`, `0003-pii-out-of-logs-and-urls.md`).

## Why
gives the project a single, ordered place to look up why a structural choice was made, separate from the per-epic acceptance docs.

## Where
docs/decisions/.
