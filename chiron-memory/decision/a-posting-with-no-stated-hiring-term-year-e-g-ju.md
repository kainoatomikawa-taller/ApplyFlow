A posting with no stated hiring term/year (e.g. just "Summer Intern" with no year) should not be excluded by a term filter — an unspecified term is treated as unknown, not as a mismatch.

## Why
Consistent with the app's existing principle (used throughout hard filters and scoring) that unknown data is never held against the candidate; otherwise a term filter would silently hide postings whose text just didn't spell out the year, indistinguishable from there being no such jobs.
